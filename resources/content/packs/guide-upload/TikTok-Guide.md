# TikTok Upload Guide

**Resolution:** 1080x1920 (9:16)
**Frame rate:** 60 FPS
**Codec:** H.264
**Bitrate:** ~8 Mbps (1080p60)
**Container:** MP4
**Duration:** up to 10 minutes
**Size limit:** 1 GB

**Pro tip:** Export at 1080p - higher resolutions get re-compressed by TikTok. Use 60 FPS for smooth motion.
